1 Core Concept

What Vantage actually is

Vantage is a strategic simulation engine for Medicare Advantage plans, ACOs, and risk-bearing provider organizations. It models the projected financial impact of interventions — clinical programs, operational changes, policy decisions — across three outcome domains simultaneously, before any dollars are committed.

The core value proposition is the Triple-Play: a single intervention (say, a pharmacist-led hypertension program) produces effects in all three financial domains at once. Risk adjustment revenue goes up as new HCCs are documented. Stars ratings improve as BP control measures close. Medical costs fall as ER visits and inpatient admissions decline. Today's siloed vendor ecosystem cannot show you all three together. Vantage can.

The One-Sentence Description
Vantage is the only platform that lets healthcare decision-makers model how a single intervention moves all three P&L levers — risk revenue, quality bonuses, and medical margin — in a single integrated simulation, in minutes instead of weeks.

Positioning

Healthcare Value Intelligence. "One Surface. One Conversation." Vantage sits at the predictive and prescriptive end of the analytics maturity spectrum — where competition is thinnest and the value premium is highest. Incumbent vendors (Cotiviti, Inovalon, Optum) own the descriptive and diagnostic tiers. Vantage plays where they don't.

Primary Market

Medicare Advantage is the target market because it is the purest expression of value-based care: capitation with full risk, where the three Vantage domains map one-to-one to distinct P&L line items. The initial segments are mid-size MA plans (30K–300K members) and provider-sponsored health plans — organizations that face the same strategic complexity as large plans but lack sophisticated in-house analytics. Healthcare consulting firms are the initial sales channel: they already serve these plans and buy tools quickly.

2 Outcome Domains

Four domains. Three revenue lines, one liability.

Outcome domains are what Vantage measures — the financial consequences of strategic decisions. The original three domains have been joined by a fourth: RADV audit risk, which is the liability-side counterpart to risk adjustment revenue. A plan that aggressively improves its RAF score without corresponding documentation quality creates audit exposure. Vantage models both sides of that balance sheet.

Domain 1
→ Base Capitation Revenue

Risk Adjustment

CMS-HCC models V24 and V28. Every undocumented condition is revenue unrealized. Vantage models prospective and retrospective coding programs, suspect condition identification, and the full V24→V28 transition exposure across a plan's population.

  • ICD-10 → CC → HCC mapping under V24 and V28
  • RAF scoring by segment (CNA/CND/CFA/CFD/CPA/CPD/INS/NE)
  • Dual-scoring delta analysis for V28 exposure
  • Coding gap identification and suspect condition engine
  • Program ROI: IHA, chart review, NLP, provider education
Domain 2
→ Quality Bonus Revenue

Stars / Quality

40+ HEDIS and CAHPS measures. Stars uses relative scoring via CMS clustering algorithms — cut-points shift year-to-year based on national performance distributions. This makes Stars a competitive intelligence problem, not just a measurement one. Vantage models cut-point movement and gap closure leverage simultaneously.

  • Five-population tracking: IPP / denominator / numerator / exclusion / exception
  • Gap status, closure probability, and reachability per member per measure
  • Stars composite projection with CMS clustering simulation
  • Cut-point movement modeling based on national performance trends
  • Measure prioritization by Stars weight × gap closure leverage
  • APP Plus and MSSP quality measure support alongside Stars
Domain 3
→ Medical Margin

Medical Cost / MLR

Under capitation, every dollar of unnecessary utilization directly erodes margin. Vantage models care management, site-of-service steerage, formulary optimization, and utilization management programs against a population's actual cost profile — and calculates shared savings or downside risk under VBC contracts.

  • TCOC calculation vs. CMS benchmarks by population segment
  • MLR decomposition: medical, admin, quality improvement
  • Utilization pattern simulation: ER, inpatient, post-acute, Rx
  • VBC contract modeling: MSSP, REACH, MA VBC, CPC+
  • Shared savings probability and downside risk exposure
  • Site-of-service steerage: ASC, preferred SNF, home health
Domain 4 · New
→ Audit Liability / Risk Exposure

RADV Audit Risk

RADV (Risk Adjustment Data Validation) is the CMS audit mechanism that validates submitted HCCs against medical record documentation. A plan that improves RAF revenue through coding programs without corresponding documentation quality creates material audit exposure. Most vendors model the revenue side; Vantage models both sides of the risk adjustment balance sheet.

  • Documentation completeness scoring per HCC per member
  • Documentation specificity tracking (high / medium / low)
  • Encounter-to-documentation lag as audit risk signal
  • Audit risk score aggregation at plan and cohort level
  • RAF revenue vs. audit exposure net position modeling
  • Coding program ROI adjusted for audit risk
Cross-Domain Lever
→ Affects All Three P&L Lines

Prior Authorization — The Gating Mechanism

PA is not a peer outcome domain — it is an intervention lever whose effects cascade through all three domains simultaneously. Approve an imaging study and you may capture a new HCC (Risk). Approve preventive services and you close quality measure gaps (Stars). Relax PA thresholds for ACO-attributed members and you increase utilization but partially recover through VBC shared savings (Cost). PA policy configuration is the most cross-domain lever in the framework, which is why it warrants its own typed parameter model.

  • Service-level PA configuration: CPT/HCPCS codes requiring authorization
  • Approval rate targets: in-network, out-of-network, ACO-attributed
  • VBC modulation: dynamic threshold adjustment based on contract performance
  • Denial policy: criteria type (MCG/InterQual/custom), peer-to-peer availability
  • Turnaround time: standard vs. expedited, auto-approval rules
  • Appeal configuration and downstream RADV documentation alignment
3 Intervention Levers

Levers are what you dial.

Intervention levers are operational inputs — things a plan or provider organization can decide to do or change. The simulation engine connects each lever to outcome domains through evidence-based effect models. Lever configurations are stored in the scenario.lever_configs table with a typed JSON parameters field that varies by lever_type.

Coding Programs

In-home assessments, chart reviews, NLP-assisted coding, provider education. Directly captures undocumented HCCs. Secondary effect on documentation quality (RADV).

Diabetes CDM, CHF management, hypertension programs. Improves coding specificity, closes quality measure gaps, reduces high-cost utilization for target conditions.

4 Agents & Skills

Claude Code as runtime, not just tooling.

Vantage's architecture is Claude-native: Claude Code is both the development environment and the production runtime. The SKILL.md files and CLAUDE.md configuration used to build the platform are the same artifacts users interact through. This eliminates infrastructure complexity while enabling the full range of conversational AI capabilities.

CLAUDE.md Three-Level Hierarchy

── CONFIGURATION HIERARCHY ───────────────────────────────────────────
~/.claude/CLAUDE.md               ← USER LEVEL
  PHI safety rules (never retrieve, display, or log real member data)
  SQL/Python preferences
  DuckDB close-before-write pattern
  Output format defaults

./CLAUDE.md                       ← PROJECT LEVEL (Vantage orchestrator)
  Intent routing logic → skill selection
  Cross-domain synthesis rules
  Population context management across conversation turns
  Triple-Play output requirements (always surface all 3 domains)
  Scenario state management
  Confidence interval and uncertainty communication standards

./skills/*/SKILL.md               ← SKILL LEVEL (one per domain/capability)
  Detailed operational instructions per model
  Domain-specific calculation logic
  Evidence weights and calibration notes
  Output format for that skill's results

quality-model

Evaluates eCQM/HEDIS measures using five-population tracking. Projects Stars using CMS clustering methodology. Models cut-point movement. Supports APP Plus and MSSP quality.

💲

Calculates TCOC against benchmarks, models utilization changes, projects MLR. Handles VBC contract performance modeling, shared savings calculations, and downside risk exposure.

🔬

scenario-runner

Activates intervention levers, routes impacts through all domain models simultaneously, produces Triple-Play summaries and ROI calculations with confidence intervals.

📅

temporal-projector

Multi-year scenario projection accounting for member churn, condition progression, compounding intervention effects, and year-over-year Stars cut-point drift.

comparator

Side-by-side scenario comparison, normalized ROI ranking across scenarios, sensitivity analysis, and executive summary generation for Board/CFO-level communication.

Critical Design Principle
The orchestrator (project-level CLAUDE.md) must enforce cross-domain synthesis even when the user asks a single-domain question. If someone asks "how does this PA policy change affect our Stars rating?", Vantage should surface the Risk and Cost implications too — because the user is making a decision that affects all three P&L lines even if they only asked about one.
5 Data Model

Four schemas, cleanly separated, designed for simulation.

DuckDB is the data layer for development and early deployment, behind a DataStore abstraction designed for future migration to Databricks or Snowflake without changing skill logic. All member identifiers in simulation are synthetic — the population.members table carries a PHI prohibition comment as a code-level reminder.

── SCHEMA: reference — stable CMS/NCQA specifications ───────────────
-- CMS-HCC crosswalk: ICD-10 → CC → HCC for both model years
CREATE TABLE reference.hcc_crosswalk (
  icd10_code      VARCHAR,
  cc_number       INTEGER,
  hcc_number      INTEGER,    -- NULL if CC but not a payment-relevant HCC
  model_year      SMALLINT,   -- 24 | 28
  effective_date  DATE
);

CREATE TABLE reference.raf_coefficients (
  hcc_number      INTEGER,
  model_year      SMALLINT,
  segment         VARCHAR,    -- CNA | CND | CFA | CFD | CPA | CPD | INS | NE
  coefficient     DECIMAL(8,4)
);

-- eCQM measure specs — five-population model aligned to DEQM IG
CREATE TABLE reference.measure_specs (
  measure_id      VARCHAR,    -- e.g. 'CMS165v12'
  measure_name    VARCHAR,
  steward         VARCHAR,    -- NCQA | CMS | AMA
  canonical_url   VARCHAR,    -- FHIR canonical for CQL library
  stars_domain    VARCHAR,    -- Clinical | Experience | Access | Part D
  stars_weight    DECIMAL(4,2),
  effective_year  SMALLINT
);

CREATE TABLE reference.pa_criteria_templates (
  service_code    VARCHAR,    -- CPT / HCPCS
  service_desc    VARCHAR,
  pa_required     BOOLEAN,
  criteria_type   VARCHAR,    -- MCG | InterQual | custom
  vbc_modifiable  BOOLEAN     -- can VBC status modulate this requirement?
);

-- Intervention effect library: lever → domain → evidence-based effect weights
CREATE TABLE reference.effect_weights (
  lever_type         VARCHAR,
  domain             VARCHAR,      -- risk | quality | cost | radv
  effect_metric      VARCHAR,      -- e.g. 'hcc_capture_rate', 'bp_control_delta'
  base_effect        DECIMAL(8,4),
  effect_low         DECIMAL(8,4), -- 10th percentile
  effect_high        DECIMAL(8,4), -- 90th percentile
  unit_description   VARCHAR,
  source_citation    VARCHAR,
  confidence         VARCHAR,      -- high | medium | low
  sdoh_modifier_json VARCHAR       -- JSON or NULL; geographic/demographic modifiers
                                   -- Populated for: cdm (SVI tier → engagement rate),
                                   --   coding_program (dual_pct tier → HCC capture rate),
                                   --   steerage (rural/urban → ASC cost reduction)
                                   -- NULL for: pa_policy, formulary, provider_contract,
                                   --   benefit_design, v28_response
);
── SCHEMA: population — the simulation subjects (always synthetic) ────
CREATE TABLE population.members (
  member_id         VARCHAR PRIMARY KEY,  -- synthetic, never real PHI
  birth_year        SMALLINT,
  sex               CHAR(1),
  dual_status       BOOLEAN,
  orec              SMALLINT,   -- original reason for entitlement code
  county_fips       VARCHAR,
  attribution_pcp   VARCHAR,    -- NPI of attributed PCP
  aco_attributed    BOOLEAN,
  enrollment_start  DATE,
  enrollment_end    DATE         -- NULL = currently enrolled
);

CREATE TABLE population.member_hccs (
  member_id    VARCHAR,
  hcc_number   INTEGER,
  model_year   SMALLINT,
  source       VARCHAR,  -- claims | chart_review | iha | suspect | nlp
  confidence   DECIMAL(4,3)  -- 1.0 = confirmed; <1.0 = suspect
);

-- PA events: connects the PA lever to RAF documentation and RADV exposure
CREATE TABLE population.pa_events (
  pa_event_id      VARCHAR PRIMARY KEY,
  member_id        VARCHAR,
  service_code     VARCHAR,    -- CPT/HCPCS
  diagnosis_codes  VARCHAR[],  -- ICD-10 codes supporting the request
  request_date     DATE,
  disposition      VARCHAR,    -- approved | denied | partial | withdrawn
  auth_period      DATERANGE,
  denial_reason    VARCHAR,
  da_vinci_ref     VARCHAR     -- FHIR Claim.id from PAS IG (future)
);
── SCHEMA: scenario — simulation configurations ──────────────────────
CREATE TABLE scenario.scenarios (
  scenario_id    VARCHAR PRIMARY KEY,
  name           VARCHAR,
  description    VARCHAR,
  population_id  VARCHAR,   -- which population snapshot
  created_at     TIMESTAMP,
  status         VARCHAR    -- draft | running | complete
);

-- Lever configurations: typed JSON parameters per lever_type
CREATE TABLE scenario.lever_configs (
  lever_id       VARCHAR PRIMARY KEY,
  scenario_id    VARCHAR,
  lever_type     VARCHAR,   -- coding_program | cdm | pa_policy | formulary |
                             --   steerage | provider_contract | benefit_design | v28_response
  parameters     JSON,      -- typed by lever_type (see lever_type specs)
  target_cohort  VARCHAR   -- SQL predicate or cohort_id
);

-- PA policy parameters (lever_type = 'pa_policy'):
-- { "services": ["CPT99213"], "approval_rate": 0.92,
--   "aco_rate": 0.95, "vbc_modulation": true,
--   "vbc_threshold_pct": 0.95, "turnaround_hours": 72,
--   "peer_to_peer": true, "auto_approve_criteria": "..." }

CREATE TABLE scenario.contract_configs (
  contract_id      VARCHAR PRIMARY KEY,
  scenario_id      VARCHAR,
  contract_type    VARCHAR,   -- mssp | reach | ma_vbc | cpci
  benchmark_pct    DECIMAL(5,3),
  savings_share    DECIMAL(4,3),
  quality_gates    JSON       -- computable quality measure conditions
);
── SCHEMA: output — simulation results ───────────────────────────────
CREATE TABLE output.member_raf (
  scenario_id    VARCHAR,
  member_id      VARCHAR,
  model_year     SMALLINT,   -- 24 | 28
  raf_baseline   DECIMAL(8,4),
  raf_projected  DECIMAL(8,4),
  delta_revenue  DECIMAL(10,2)
);

-- Five-population quality model: aligned to DEQM / Da Vinci IG
CREATE TABLE output.measure_populations (
  scenario_id      VARCHAR,
  member_id        VARCHAR,
  measure_id       VARCHAR,
  population_type  VARCHAR   -- IPP | denominator | numerator | exclusion | exception
);

CREATE TABLE output.measure_gaps (
  scenario_id          VARCHAR,
  member_id            VARCHAR,
  measure_id           VARCHAR,
  gap_status           BOOLEAN,    -- true = gap open (not in numerator)
  closure_probability  DECIMAL(4,3),
  reachability         VARCHAR,    -- reachable | excluded | lost_to_followup
  projected_closed     BOOLEAN     -- after lever application
);

-- RADV: the liability side of the risk adjustment balance sheet
CREATE TABLE output.member_radv_risk (
  scenario_id               VARCHAR,
  member_id                 VARCHAR,
  hcc_id                    INTEGER,
  documentation_complete    BOOLEAN,
  documentation_specificity VARCHAR,   -- high | medium | low
  encounter_to_doc_lag      INTEGER,   -- days between encounter and HCC support
  audit_risk_score          DECIMAL(4,3)
);

CREATE TABLE output.scenario_summary (
  scenario_id          VARCHAR PRIMARY KEY,
  risk_revenue_delta   DECIMAL(12,2),
  quality_bonus_delta  DECIMAL(12,2),
  medical_cost_delta   DECIMAL(12,2),
  radv_exposure_delta  DECIMAL(12,2),
  net_value            DECIMAL(12,2),
  roi_ratio            DECIMAL(6,2),
  confidence_low       DECIMAL(12,2),
  confidence_high      DECIMAL(12,2),
  break_even_month     SMALLINT
);
DuckDB Lessons from HealthSim
Close-before-write: always close read connections before opening write connections. Retry with exponential backoff for lock contention (initial wait 100ms, max 5 retries). Keep DuckDB files small through schema partitioning to avoid Git LFS. Maintain the DataStore abstraction so the entire data layer can be swapped for Databricks or Snowflake without touching skill logic.
6 Da Vinci Alignment

Structural alignment, not implementation dependency.

The HL7 Da Vinci project has produced a suite of implementation guides that define the interoperability infrastructure for the domains Vantage models. Vantage is a simulation system, not an operational system — so Da Vinci IGs are treated as sources of domain modeling insight and future data ingestion pathways, not implementation requirements for Phase 1.

The strategic value: designing internal data structures to mirror the semantic structure of these IGs means that future ingestion of real FHIR data becomes a mapping exercise rather than a schema redesign. It also creates credibility with technically sophisticated buyers who understand the Da Vinci ecosystem.

DEQM

Data Exchange for Quality Measures

Defines the five-population model (IPP, denominator, numerator, exclusion, exception) that Vantage's quality model uses internally. Aligns output.measure_populations to the canonical eCQM data model. Future: ingest DEQM measure reports from health systems directly.

CRD

Coverage Requirements Discovery

Surfaces payer coverage requirements at the point of care within clinical workflows. Relevant to PA lever simulation: the set of services requiring PA and the criteria used to evaluate requests. Informs reference.pa_criteria_templates structure.

DTR

Documentation Templates & Coverage Rules

Automates documentation gathering for PA requests using FHIR Questionnaire resources. The documentation context captured in DTR responses is the same diagnostic context that creates HCC documentation opportunities — the PA → Risk Adjustment connection.

PAS

Prior Authorization Support

Standardizes the PA submission and response workflow. Defines the FHIR Claim resource structure for PA requests. population.pa_events.da_vinci_ref stores the future Claim.id from this IG, enabling real PA data ingestion without schema changes.

CDex

Clinical Data Exchange

Enables payer-initiated data requests from providers — the mechanism for chart review and HCC documentation validation. Relevant to RADV modeling: CDex defines how a payer would request documentation to support a coded HCC in an audit scenario.

VBPR

Value-Based Performance Reporting

Defines performance reporting structures for VBC contracts. Aligns to scenario.contract_configs quality gates model. Future: ingest performance reports from VBC contracts to calibrate simulation benchmarks with real contract data.

7 Key Architectural Decisions

Decisions made, with rationale.

DecisionChoice MadeRationale
RADV as 4th domain Yes — modeled separately in output.member_radv_risk Risk adjustment revenue without corresponding documentation quality creates material audit liability. Most vendors ignore the liability side. Vantage models both, which is a genuine differentiator.
PA architectural role Cross-domain lever, not a peer outcome domain PA is a gating mechanism whose effects are measured through the three revenue domains. Elevating it to a domain would conflate inputs with outputs. It remains a lever with typed simulation parameters.
Quality model population Five-population (IPP / denominator / numerator / exclusion / exception) DEQM IG and NCQA HEDIS both use five-population logic. A binary numerator/denominator model cannot correctly handle exclusions or exceptions, which materially affects gap calculations and Stars projection accuracy.
Effect weight calibration Literature-sourced weights in reference.effect_weights with uncertainty ranges The causal connections between levers and domains require empirical grounding. Weights are stored with source citation and confidence level so simulations surface their own uncertainty honestly.
SDOH-modulated effect weights sdoh_modifier_json column on reference.effect_weights; three levers populated Population demographics and geography shift effective intervention outcomes. CDM engagement is ~22% lower in high-SVI counties. Coding yield is ~28% higher for DSNP-heavy populations. Rural ASC steerage savings are ~75% lower due to absent alternatives. The modifier is a JSON multiplier applied at simulation time using SVI tier, dual-eligible percentage, or rural/urban classification from the population profile. Five levers have NULL modifiers — evidence doesn't support geographic adjustment there. This is a differentiator: most tools use flat benchmarks regardless of who the population is.
Runtime environment Claude Code (both dev and runtime) Eliminates infrastructure complexity. The SKILL.md files built during development are the same artifacts users interact with. This is architecturally appropriate for a strategic simulation tool used in conversation.
Data layer DuckDB with DataStore abstraction DuckDB is appropriate for development and early-deployment scale. The abstraction layer ensures migration to Databricks or Snowflake is a configuration change, not a rewrite.
HealthSim relationship Selective fork — proven patterns forward, no dependency HealthSim's population generation is proven. Fork the DataStore abstraction, synthetic population generation, and DuckDB connection patterns. Leave behind HealthSim-specific domain logic. No ongoing dependency.
Da Vinci IGs Structural alignment in schema design; not an implementation requirement Aligning data model semantics to Da Vinci IGs makes future real-data ingestion a mapping exercise. Credibility signal with sophisticated buyers. No requirement to implement FHIR infrastructure in Phase 1.
GTM sequencing Consulting channel first → OSS core in parallel → Direct SaaS later Consultants buy tools quickly and have existing MA plan relationships. OSS builds community credibility in parallel. Direct SaaS to mid-market plans requires reference customers first. V28 urgency is the entry hook.
Open Architectural Question
The calibration of effect weights (the causal relationships between levers and outcome domains) is the primary technical risk. These weights require literature synthesis across clinical, actuarial, and health economics research. The mitigation approach: Claude can directly support this synthesis, producing documented weights with uncertainty ranges and source citations stored in reference.effect_weights. This is tracked as a Phase 1 parallel workstream.
