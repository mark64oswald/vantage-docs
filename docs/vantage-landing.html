The Problem

Strategy lives in silos.
The costs live in the gaps.

Medicare Advantage plans operate across three interconnected financial domains — each measured by different teams, with different tools, on different timelines. The result: no one ever sees the full picture before committing resources.

01

Siloed Analysis

Risk adjustment, quality, and cost management are served by separate vendor ecosystems that never speak to each other. A question that touches all three can take weeks to answer.

02

No Simulation Capability

Incumbent platforms (Cotiviti, Inovalon, Optum) excel at measuring what happened. None offer self-service strategic simulation of what could happen — or what should.

03

High-Stakes Blind Spots

A wrong bet on a $3M care management program can cascade into a 3-star rating and $50M+ in downstream revenue impact. Decision-makers deserve better tools than spreadsheets and hope.

Prior Authorization — The Cross-Domain Lever

PA isn't just a utilization management tool — it's a gating mechanism whose effects ripple through all three domains. Approve an imaging study and you may capture a new HCC. Approve preventive services and you improve quality measures. Relax PA for ACO-attributed members and you may reduce network churn while partially recovering cost through VBC shared savings. Vantage treats PA as a configurable simulation lever — dial the policy, see the cross-domain impact. Aligned with FHIR Da Vinci implementation guides for future real-world data integration.
